# Gameplay loop

1. Prepare soil
2. Plant
3. Grow
4. Harvest
5. Cure
6. Ferment
7. Age
8. Blend
9. Roll
10. Package
11. Sell
12. Expand
13. Repeat

## Goal

Produce the world's most respected premium cigars by mastering agriculture, processing, blending, and presentation across a persistent legacy.
