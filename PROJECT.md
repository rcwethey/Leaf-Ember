# Project

## Product definition

Build a deeply embodied craftsmanship and management RPG centered on premium cigar production. The canonical creative direction is defined in [`VISION.md`](VISION.md) and indexed by [`docs/design/README.md`](docs/design/README.md).

## Core pillars

- **Craftsmanship:** player knowledge and deliberate choices determine quality.
- **Growth without displacement:** delegation expands choice without making personal craft obsolete.
- **Patience:** meaningful processes unfold over days, seasons, and years without empty waiting.
- **Provenance:** every meaningful lot and cigar carries its agricultural and process history.
- **Legacy:** the finca, inventory, artisans, relationships, and reputation tell a persistent story.

## Target

- Unity 6 LTS (6000.5.4f1)
- C#
- Universal Render Pipeline
- Windows via Steam

## Directional constraints

- The finca is an intimate, physically represented production pipeline rather than an acreage-maximization map.
- Growing estate tobacco is important, but relationships with other growers are required for a broad blending vocabulary.
- Named artisans reproduce proven standards while the player retains access to hands-on work at every stage of progression.
- Reputation is audience-specific and emerges from products, promises, relationships, and conduct.
- Tobacco is an agricultural and artisanal material rather than a generic crafting ingredient.

## Current design focus

The next unresolved system is flavor profiles and sensory learning. Do not implement a final cigar-quality score or blend-resolution formula until that design is canonical.
