# Open design questions

**Status:** Active design queue
**Last reviewed:** 2026-08-02

This file distinguishes unresolved work from canonical direction. An open question must not be silently converted into an implementation assumption.

## Next focus: flavor profiles and sensory learning

The next design phase must establish:

- How tobacco lots represent strength, body, aroma, taste, texture, and combustion
- Which attributes are intrinsic, process-dependent, contextual, or perceived
- How blend components interact rather than merely average together
- How a cigar evolves across its beginning, middle, and final portion
- How construction changes the experienced blend
- How the player learns descriptors and improves diagnostic confidence
- How subjective feedback differs among characters and audiences
- How uncertainty is shown without making outcomes feel random
- How recipes record intent, observations, and revisions
- How to avoid reducing a cigar to one quality score

No final blend-evaluation model or flavor UI should be implemented until this system has a canonical design document.

## Subsequent design questions

### Construction

- Which construction actions are embodied and which are represented through focused interfaces?
- How are draw, burn, compression, dimensions, leaf preparation, and finishing connected?
- How does player skill differ from character skill and player knowledge?

### Character relationships

- How deep are personal schedules, dialogue, mentorship, conflict, and friendship?
- Can artisans leave, specialize, collaborate, or eventually lead parts of the house?
- Is romance part of the intended game or outside its scope?

### Setting specifics

- Is the fictional valley located within real-world Nicaragua or a fictional country grounded primarily in northern Nicaragua?
- What contemporary year or range best supports the intended technology and market?
- What Spanish-language presentation and localization scope is appropriate?

### Business and market

- Which sales channels are directly playable?
- How are export, regulations, packaging, taxes, and contracts represented without overwhelming the craft?
- What distinguishes commercial success from cultural or critical prestige?

### Calendar

- Exact action costs, season length, travel treatment, and time-advance limits
- How long aging can remain meaningful within a playable career
- Whether and how the game depicts multiple years and succession

### Player embodiment

- Final camera and interaction model for the finca
- Which close-up activities require dedicated workbench interfaces
- Character customization and personal history options
