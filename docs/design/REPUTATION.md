# Reputation and relationships

**Status:** Canonical direction; detailed progression values remain open
**Last reviewed:** 2026-08-02

## Core rule

Reputation is not one number and quality is not a universal consensus.

The house becomes known through repeated work, fulfilled promises, public releases, private relationships, and the way it treats people. Different audiences notice different things and may disagree about the same cigar.

## Reputation audiences

Potential audiences include:

- **Growers:** Fair dealing, respect for provenance, reliable contracts, and thoughtful use of their leaf
- **Artisans:** Working conditions, teaching, standards, creative respect, and opportunities to grow
- **Buyers and exporters:** Reliability, consistency, communication, demand, and commercial judgment
- **Critics and connoisseurs:** Construction, complexity, originality, evolution, and coherence of intent
- **Collectors and private clients:** Scarcity, story, aging potential, provenance, and personal access
- **Regular customers:** Value, availability, consistency, and recognizable character
- **Local community:** Employment, conduct, participation, and the house's relationship to the region
- **Traditionalists and experimenters:** Different tolerances for innovation, continuity, and unusual technique

These groups can overlap without sharing identical priorities.

## House identity

The game should infer a recognizable identity from the player's behavior rather than asking them to select a permanent brand class.

A house may become known as:

- Precise and consistent
- Bold, strong, or uncompromising
- Elegant and restrained
- Experimental and unpredictable
- Deeply tied to its estate tobacco
- Exceptional at aging
- A producer of difficult small batches
- A dependable commercial partner
- A respected school for artisans

Identity creates expectations. Fulfilling, challenging, or betraying those expectations has consequences.

## Personal and institutional reputation

The player and the cigar house have related but distinct reputations.

- Personally crafted atelier releases can strengthen the player's standing as a maker.
- Staff-produced lines demonstrate whether the house can preserve standards without constant intervention.
- A famous founder with an unreliable operation is different from a trusted house whose artisans are respected in their own right.
- Credit for an exceptional cigar may belong to the player, a named artisan, the growing team, or a collaboration.

This distinction makes succession, mentorship, and delegation emotionally meaningful.

## How reputation develops

Reputation should come from concrete evidence:

- The consistency of delivered batches
- Whether a release matches its stated intent
- Construction and storage performance
- Critical and customer response over time
- Honesty about provenance and substitutions
- Contract fulfillment and payment behavior
- Treatment and development of employees
- Responses to mistakes, recalls, and disappointing crops
- Invitations, commissions, collaborations, and repeat orders

Marketing can shape awareness and expectations, but it cannot permanently substitute for the work.

## Feedback

Feedback arrives through people and situations rather than a single end-of-day score:

- Workshop tastings
- Buyer conversations
- Artisan observations
- Letters and order reports
- Reviews and awards
- Returns or construction complaints
- Repeat purchases and waiting lists
- Invitations to events or collaborations
- Growers offering access to better lots

Feedback can be incomplete, subjective, biased, or contradictory. The player learns to interpret the source as well as the message.

## Design constraints

- Never collapse reputation into one universal star rating.
- Do not make every audience prefer maximum strength, rarity, price, or complexity.
- Do not make a prestigious label erase poor construction or broken commitments.
- Allow a small house to be deeply respected without becoming a mass producer.
- Make mistakes recoverable through honest action and sustained improvement.
- Let named characters remember consequential choices.

The flavor system establishes how cigars are experienced and discussed, while [`MARKET_AND_ECONOMY.md`](MARKET_AND_ECONOMY.md) defines releases, accounts, and sell-through. [`CHARACTERS_AND_RELATIONSHIPS.md`](CHARACTERS_AND_RELATIONSHIPS.md) defines the named people, memories, commitments, and role-specific confidence through which reputation becomes personal. Exact reputation values and progression remain prototype work.
